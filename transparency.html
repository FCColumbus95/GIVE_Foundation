Nonprofit identity & tax status

Greater G.I.V.E. Foundation traces its history to Tri Village Soccer Association (TVSA), with annual service initiatives since 1995 and a transition to the Greater GIVE name and expanded mission in 2024–2025.

Tri Village Soccer Association was incorporated on May 13, 1994. The name changed to Greater GIVE Foundation on November 17, 2024, with the same EIN, 31-1460452, retained. The Foundation has confirmed these identity and history details. The IRS Exempt Organizations Business Master File lists TRI-VILLAGE SOCCER ASSOCIATION under EIN 31-1460452 with a ruling date of September 1996 (recorded as 199609), subsection 501(c)(3), unconditional exemption status, and deductible-contribution classification. These fields were checked against the IRS Ohio data file on November 22, 2026. The ruling date is a month and year; no exact ruling day is inferred. No IRS application or reinstatement is represented as pending.

IRS record and name-change documentation: the IRS extract checked still uses TVSA’s name and older contact information. The Foundation’s reported November 17, 2024 name change is stated separately; this page does not claim that the IRS has already updated its record to Greater GIVE Foundation. The determination letter and supporting name-change records remain to be published.

Source: IRS Ohio exempt-organization data (CSV), available through the IRS Business Master File download page. The IRS field guide explains the ruling-date and status codes.

Listed U.S. principal office: Greater G.I.V.E. Foundation, 4395 Weaver Ct N, Hilliard, Ohio 43026, USA. Contact: donations@greatergivefoundation.org.

Federal exemption, state charitable-solicitation registration, and recognition in other countries are separate matters. Current registration or exemption records must be confirmed for applicable jurisdictions. The future Kitson Town Food Cooperative is not currently operating.

Jamaica registration

Greater GIVE Foundation is not yet a registered charity in Jamaica. The registration process is underway and is not complete. This status was confirmed by the Foundation on November 22, 2026. No completed Jamaican charity registration, registration number, approval date, or Jamaican tax benefit is represented on this website.

The Jamaica leadership group is listed as proposed branch directors. A Jamaica contact address does not establish charitable registration. Confirm the legal recipient and applicable tax treatment before contributing; U.S. status does not by itself establish Jamaican status.

Official guidance is available from Jamaica’s Department of Co-operatives and Friendly Societies. Registration evidence will be added when approval is complete and the public record is available.

Donation information & terms

Charitable purposes: education, scholarships, youth development, school supplies, nutrition, assistance to elders and people with disabilities, and community infrastructure.

Online donations are available through the Foundation’s Zeffy campaign, embedded in the donation section and available as a separate page. Review the recipient, amount, payment details, any recurring schedule, and total in Zeffy before submitting. Opening the form or selecting Donate on this website does not itself authorize a payment.

Tax treatment: the deductibility of a contribution depends on verified recipient eligibility, applicable law, and any goods or services received. Program fees, merchandise, and event payments are not automatically charitable gifts. This website does not promise a particular tax result.

Payment-platform notices: Zeffy may display a tax-receipt message. A platform-generated receipt does not independently verify recipient eligibility or guarantee a deduction in the donor’s jurisdiction. Check the recipient’s current official status and any benefits received before relying on a tax deduction.

Receipts: contact the Foundation to request or correct an acknowledgment. Provide the date, amount, and payment reference through an appropriate channel; never email complete card or account numbers. Acknowledgments must accurately describe any benefits received in exchange. The Foundation does not assign the donor’s claimed tax value to noncash gifts.

Errors, refunds, and recurring gifts: contact the Foundation promptly about an incorrect donation or refund request. For a recurring gift, refer to the management instructions provided through Zeffy and contact the Foundation if you need help. Review the terms shown by Zeffy before submitting. This notice does not create a universal refund entitlement.

Fees and optional platform contributions: review all amounts shown in Zeffy, including any optional contribution to the platform, before authorizing payment. This website does not claim that a donor’s entire checkout total is received by the Foundation.

Project preferences: an inquiry or selected project does not establish a restricted-gift agreement. Confirm acceptance and any conditions before giving. Posted project costs are planning estimates, not promises of completion or statements of funds raised.

Privacy notice

This notice describes the current informational website and how visitors can raise privacy concerns. It covers inquiries, donor-related communications, and published material. It must be revised when services or collection practices change.

Information you provide

The website includes a third-party Zeffy donation form and links to the Foundation’s Zeffy campaign. Information entered into the donation form is submitted through Zeffy. The Foundation may receive donor and transaction information through its fundraising account to administer donations, acknowledgments, and related requests. Payment credentials are entered in Zeffy’s payment flow rather than a Foundation-built form. The website has no separate Foundation account-registration form, newsletter signup, program application, or child-registration form. Email links open your own email application; the Foundation receives information you choose to send.

Messages can be used to answer the inquiry and administer the related request. Donation records, if provided or received through external payment arrangements, may also be needed for acknowledgments, bookkeeping, and legal obligations. Do not send sensitive beneficiary information, bank credentials, or complete payment-card details through ordinary email.

Hosting, access, and technical information

The website uses hosting, security, and delivery services, which may include Cloudflare technical measurement or analytics. The donation section loads Zeffy’s external embed script and form. Zeffy and its service providers may process technical and transaction information under their own privacy notices, including any cookies or similar technologies used by those services. Hosting and access providers may also process IP addresses, timestamps, requested pages, security logs, and sign-in information. This notice does not claim that third-party services use no cookies.

Service providers and sharing

Zeffy provides the embedded donation form and campaign page; its privacy notice and terms are available through the donation experience. Email, hosting, and payment services process information under their own arrangements and notices. The Foundation’s internal access, sharing, and retention practices remain subject to its operating policies. No promise of absolute confidentiality or an unverified claim that information is never shared is made here.

Retention and requests

Contact donations@greatergivefoundation.org to ask about information you supplied, request a correction or deletion where applicable, or ask to stop optional communications. Describe the request without sending sensitive identity documents. Requests may require proportionate identity verification, and some records may need to be retained for tax, accounting, legal, or security purposes.

Retention decisions must take account of the purpose of the record, tax and accounting duties, applicable legal requirements, disputes, and security needs; deletion requests may not override those obligations. A category-specific retention schedule, a designated privacy lead, and approved request and incident-response procedures remain to be confirmed. This page does not represent those internal policies as already adopted. The Foundation does not promise absolute security or rights beyond those available under applicable law.

Children, photographs & stories

Parents and guardians should handle inquiries and participation arrangements for children. Do not send children’s medical, school, identity, or other sensitive records through the general email address.

Program enrollment and collection of information about children require an appropriate, separate process. Identifiable beneficiary photographs and stories must be handled with suitable permissions, dignity, and safeguarding. For children, establish appropriate parent or guardian authorization before publication.

The opening image is AI-created representative imagery. Campaign artwork is distinct from documentary project photographs. For concerns about a photograph, story, or permission, contact the Foundation and identify the page and image. The Foundation must verify existing releases and approve a documented consent and removal process. Publication of an image does not itself prove that a release has been verified. Identify a concern through the contact link without resending sensitive child records.

Concerns & complaints

For donation errors, privacy requests, safeguarding concerns, or concerns about fundraising accuracy, contact donations@greatergivefoundation.org or write to Greater GIVE Foundation, 4395 Weaver Ct N, Hilliard, Ohio 43026, USA. Use a brief subject such as “Privacy request,” “Donation concern,” or “Safeguarding concern.”

Include the relevant page or activity, a concise description, and a safe way to contact you. Do not email complete card numbers, medical or identity records, or sensitive information about a child. Ask for a suitable way to share sensitive details. This general mailbox is not an anonymous, confidential, or continuously monitored reporting service.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. This website is not an emergency reporting channel. The Foundation still needs to designate a safeguarding lead and an independent escalation route, including for concerns involving a director. A formal investigation procedure, response targets, and a non-retaliation policy require board approval; they are not represented here as already operational.

Governance & policy review

The Board of Directors is identified on the home page. Board membership does not by itself establish the adoption or implementation of the policies listed below. No adoption dates or compliance certifications are inferred.

Policies requiring documented confirmation or approval

  • Conflicts of interest: disclosure of relevant interests and relationships, recusal from affected decisions, and a record of independent approval.
  • Financial controls: board-approved budgets, appropriate separation of duties, payment authorization, reconciliations, expense documentation, and review of financial reports.
  • Gift acceptance and restricted funds: acceptance criteria, written restrictions, donor communications, separate tracking, and procedures when a project changes.
  • Safeguarding and conduct: protection of children and vulnerable adults, suitable volunteer screening, boundaries, reporting, escalation, and prevention of exploitation, abuse, and harassment.
  • Complaints and whistleblowing: accessible reporting, impartial review, protection against retaliation, and a route outside the person named in a complaint.
  • Privacy and records: assigned responsibilities, access controls, retention, secure disposal, service-provider arrangements, and incident response.
  • Photographs and stories: informed permission, parent or guardian authorization where appropriate, dignity, careful identification, and removal requests.
  • International partnerships: proportionate partner checks, written responsibilities, documented transfers, monitoring of intended use, and applicable local requirements.

These are review requirements, not representations that the board has already adopted a policy. Approved public versions or summaries should carry their adoption and review dates. Private personnel, beneficiary, donor, and security records should not be published.

Results and financial reporting

Project reporting should identify the period, location, activity, beneficiaries or other measures, and the source of the figures. Planning budgets should remain distinct from money raised, expenditure, and completed work. Financial statements and annual reports should state their reporting year and whether they are internally prepared, independently reviewed, or audited.

Accountability benchmarks

Legal requirements depend on where an organization is established, operates, solicits funds, or processes personal information. There is no claim here of blanket compliance with every country’s laws or all international charitable standards.

The BBB Standards for Charity Accountability provide a voluntary benchmark for governance, finances, results, and fundraising transparency. The Core Humanitarian Standard provides a relevant framework for dignity, participation, safeguarding, complaints, and accountability in assistance work. Listing these resources does not claim that GGF is accredited, certified, endorsed, or verified against either framework.

Privacy and fundraising notices must match actual activities and the laws that apply to them. International availability of this website does not by itself establish registration or tax benefits abroad.

Contacting the Foundation does not create a donation, enrollment, volunteer appointment, or contract. Separate confirmed terms apply to those arrangements. Third-party services have their own terms and privacy notices. Nothing here limits rights that cannot lawfully be limited.
