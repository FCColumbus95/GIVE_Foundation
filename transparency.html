Organization & public records

Greater G.I.V.E. Foundation lists its U.S. head office at 4395 Weaver Ct N, Hilliard, Ohio 43026. The Foundation’s exact IRS legal name, EIN, current federal tax status, and charitable solicitation registration must be verified before this website represents contributions as tax-deductible. This notice does not indicate that an IRS application is pending or that recognition has been denied.

To request available public exemption application materials, an IRS determination letter, or annual tax filings subject to public disclosure, contact donations@greatergivefoundation.org or write to the U.S. head office. Applicable inspection and copying rights remain available under law; this website does not replace them. Public copies must exclude only information legally permitted to be withheld. Donor disclosure rules differ for public charities and private foundations.

The proposed Jamaica branch and any separately organized enterprise must be distinguished from the U.S. entity. U.S. tax status does not by itself establish registration or tax treatment in another country.

Giving information

The charitable purposes presented here are education, practical training, school supplies, food and care assistance, scholar-athlete development, and community infrastructure. Confirm the receiving legal entity, intended use, and verified payment channel before transferring funds.

No donation is collected through this website. The amount and frequency controls express an inquiry only; they do not authorize a charge, establish a monthly subscription, or reserve a restricted gift. Never email card numbers, bank credentials, or identity documents.

Do not rely on this website as confirmation of deductibility. A gift’s tax treatment depends on the receiving organization’s verified status, the nature of the payment, any benefit received, and applicable law. Program fees, merchandise, and event admission are not automatically charitable gifts.

Before a future checkout is enabled, it must identify the legal recipient and currency, display any fees and total charge, explain restrictions and refunds, and provide explicit recurring-payment consent, billing frequency, and a working cancellation method. These terms must be approved and implemented before accepting payment.

For questions about an existing contribution, an error, an acknowledgment, or recurring payments arranged elsewhere, contact the Foundation using the contact details above. This draft does not create a refund guarantee or alter an existing gift agreement.

Privacy notice

This static website has no account registration, online payment form, newsletter signup, or child registration form. Its supplied code does not install analytics, advertising trackers, or browser storage. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs; the deployed host’s actual practices must be confirmed before launch.

Choosing an email link opens your own email application. If you send a message, the Foundation receives the information you choose to provide, including your email address and message. Send only information needed for your inquiry. Do not send sensitive information about a child or beneficiary through ordinary email.

Use the Foundation’s contact details to ask about personal information you have supplied, request correction or deletion where applicable, or ask to stop optional communications. Some records may need to be retained for tax, accounting, legal, or security purposes. Before launch, the Foundation must approve its actual retention, sharing, service-provider, and request-handling practices; no unverified promise to never share data is made here.

Parents and guardians should handle program inquiries for children. Participation forms and identifiable photographs or stories of children require a separate, appropriate consent and safeguarding process before collection or publication. External websites, email services, and future payment processors have their own privacy practices. This notice must be updated before new collection or tracking features are introduced.
