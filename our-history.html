Where our story begins

Scholar-athletic development
in Central Ohio.

Greater GIVE Foundation was previously Tri Village Soccer Association (TVSA), a soccer-specific organization supporting scholar-athletic development initiatives in Central Ohio.

Beginning in 1995, the organization carried out annual initiatives that connected participation in sport with education, opportunity, and practical support. These included a scholarship program for high schoolers transitioning to college and scholarships that enabled youths with limited financial resources to participate in travel soccer.

The organization also donated equipment, jerseys, shorts, socks, warm-ups, cleats, and soccer balls to communities in Colombia, Zambia, and Jamaica. These initiatives extended the reach of its service beyond Central Ohio while maintaining a commitment to youth development.

Our journey

Continuity through change.

May 13, 1994

TVSA incorporated

Tri Village Soccer Association was incorporated on May 13, 1994. This incorporation date is distinct from the IRS ruling date of September 1996.

Since 1995

Annual service through sport and education

College-transition scholarships, travel-soccer scholarships, and equipment donations became annual initiatives supporting scholar-athletic development in Central Ohio and internationally.

September 1996

IRS charitable recognition

The IRS Business Master File records a September 1996 ruling date for TRI-VILLAGE SOCCER ASSOCIATION, EIN 31-1460452. The record checked November 22, 2026 lists 501(c)(3) classification, unconditional exemption, and deductible contributions. View the IRS source and name-change disclosure.

November 17, 2024

The transition to Greater GIVE Foundation

The organization changed its name to Greater GIVE Foundation on November 17, 2024, retaining EIN 31-1460452. The board expanded its mission into an international initiative serving the global population. Greater GIVE Foundation carries that broader purpose forward.

A continuing scholar-athletic tradition

From Santos FC
to FC Columbus.

TVSA was a league sponsor for Santos FC, which became FC Columbus.

FC Columbus continues the tradition of helping scholar athletes develop and grow toward fulfillment of their purpose in life. Greater GIVE Foundation continues to support youth sports as a means of transforming lives and assisting those in need.

This shared history connects education, athletic development, and service across generations.

An expanded mission

More communities.
A continuing commitment.

The transition to Greater GIVE Foundation broadened the organization’s scope while carrying forward its tradition of giving.

Support for children, elders, education, youth sports, and community development now forms part of an international initiative. Immediate assistance and long-term capacity building express the same purpose: helping individuals and communities develop, grow, and thrive.
